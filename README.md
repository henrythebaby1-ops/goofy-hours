# Goofy Hours

A silly first-person spooky shooter that runs in the browser. Three not-very-scary places, one foam dart blaster, way too much confetti.

**Play:** https://henrythebaby1-ops.github.io/goofy-hours/

- **The Beige Halls**: an endless office haunted by intern ghosts who want to know if you got their email. Find 3 rubber ducks.
- **Pizza Party Palace**: a closed party arcade. The robot mascots freeze when your flashlight is on them. Find 3 pizza slices.
- **The Snooze Ward**: sleepwalkers everywhere. Drink coffee or the dream sheep show up. Find 3 alarm clocks.

Controls: WASD to walk, mouse to look, click to shoot, Shift to run, R to reload, F for flashlight, P to pause. Works on phones with touch controls.

One file, no build step: just `index.html`.
